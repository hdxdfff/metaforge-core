# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Treat people with
respect, focus feedback on the work, and do not publish another person's
private information without permission.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual
language or imagery in community spaces, and sustained disruption of technical
discussion.

Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments
that violate this standard. Report concerns through GitHub's private reporting
channel when available. Do not open a public issue for an urgent safety or
security matter.

This policy applies in all project spaces and whenever someone represents the
project publicly.
